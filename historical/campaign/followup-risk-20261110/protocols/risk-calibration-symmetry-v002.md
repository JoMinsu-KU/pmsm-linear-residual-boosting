## Motivation and fixed question

The v001 comparison gave a constant shift to MSE and convex mixing weights to asym4/q90. On exposed Paderborn evaluation, the selected asymmetric mix had lower RMSE/overMAE than shifted MSE but higher under-RMS. Neither dominated the other. The narrow additional question is whether **asym4 plus exactly the same calibration procedure** reduces under-RMS beyond MSE plus calibration at the common diagnostic budgets. The q90 plus calibration control is mandatory. Gating C17 is a separate experiment.

Primary contrast is raw asym4+shift versus raw MSE+shift for each paired neural seed. Do not choose between raw asym4 and the v001 mixture based on evaluation. Do not add a two-dimensional weight/shift search. Tree MSE/q90 controls receive the same procedure; HGB has no asymmetric fit and cannot support a three-loss claim.

## Calibration and selection

For each component separately, use shifts [0,.1,.2,.3,.4,.5,.75,1,1.25,1.5,2,2.5,3,4,5,7.5,10] degrees C plus max(0,q) from that component's own **CAL-only**, profile-balanced signed residual quantiles [.5,.6,.7,.8,.9,.95]. Use the v001 sorted weighted empirical inverse CDF, preserving the first occurrence of exact duplicate shifts. Quantile-derived shifts may exceed10°C just as in v001; do not introduce a post-result cap. No negative shift is permitted.

For every loss sharing a backbone/seed, the budget denominator is the **same raw MSE component's CAL macro metrics**, not its own raw metric. Primary limits remain macroRMSE<=1.05×rawMSE and macrooverMAE<=1.25×rawMSE. Evaluate every original16 budget pair. Select the feasible setting with smallest macro under-RMS; ties lower macroRMSE, lower macrooverMAE, then declared setting order. Numerical inequality tolerance1e-12 only. When no setting is feasible, selected_setting_id is null and status is NO_FEASIBLE_CAL_SETTING. Do not substitute MSE and label it asymmetric, and do not average only feasible seeds as a complete three-seed result.

The same candidate-building and calibration-selection rule applies to MSE/asym4/q90. Own residual quantiles differ numerically but are generated by the same procedure. All settings, unselected candidates, null selections and results remain visible. Raw contextual D9/C15/linear33 predictions may be reported but are not candidates in the primary neural comparison.
